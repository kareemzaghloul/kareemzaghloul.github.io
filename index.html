§ 01 — About

Practical CAD modeling built around manufacturability.

I am Kareem Zaghloul, a Computer-Aided Manufacturing student at Al-Balqa Applied University in Jordan. My focus is mechanical CAD design, structured 3D assemblies, and clear 2D engineering documentation.

Rather than treating CAD as isolated surface geometry, I approach each part and assembly with machining access, fastening hardware, fit, and drawing clarity in mind.

This portfolio documents a structured 7-project progression: starting with a completed industrial sensor mounting assembly and expanding through reverse engineering, sheet metal, workholding fixtures, linkages, gearboxes, and fluid machinery.

§ 04 — Engineering Process

The workflow I apply to each project.

  1. STEP 01

    Define requirements & constraints

    Clarify functional goals, mounting interfaces, adjustment travel, and hardware standards before modeling.

  2. STEP 02

    Concept & sketching

    Evaluate geometry layouts and component orientation to balance simplicity, stiffness, and access.

  3. STEP 03

    Parametric part modeling

    Build clean, editable SOLIDWORKS feature trees with clear design intent and reference planes.

  4. STEP 04

    Assembly & digital verification

    Constrain components with realistic mates, verify range of motion, and run interference detection.

  5. STEP 05

    Technical drawings & documentation

    Produce orthographic part sheets, exploded views, and structured case-study documentation.

  6. STEP 06

    Manufacturability review

    Check machining access, tool clearance, and assembly sequence before finalizing the design package.
